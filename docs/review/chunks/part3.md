## Part 3 · School pressure (H2)

The school-pressure hypothesis predicts that adolescent distress rose when and where academic demands intensified: during the high-stakes high-school years, in states that introduced test-based accountability reforms, and in countries where students report rising schoolwork pressure. We test this channel using U.S. adolescent time-use diaries, the cross-national HBSC schoolwork-pressure series, and state accountability policy timelines.
